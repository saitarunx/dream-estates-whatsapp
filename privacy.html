Privacy Policy

Dream Estates · Last updated: 28 September 2026

Dream Estates ("we", "us") sells residential plots in Ghatkesar and Adibatla, Hyderabad, Telangana. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you contact us on WhatsApp, through our ads or through our website, how we use it, and your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. What we collect

  • Contact details: your name and phone number (including your WhatsApp number).
  • Your enquiry: the messages you send us and the answers you choose, such as preferred location, budget, plot size, purchase timeline and site-visit slot.
  • Ad and form details: if you reach us through a Facebook or Instagram ad or lead form, the details you enter in that form and which ad you came from.

We do not collect payment details, Aadhaar, PAN or other identity documents through WhatsApp or our website.

2. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and send you the plot details, prices and brochure you asked for.
  • To book, confirm and remind you about site visits.
  • To follow up about your enquiry. A member of our team reviews every lead before we message you.
  • To understand which of our ads work, using totals and counts rather than individual profiles.

We do not sell your data and do not share it with other builders or agents.

3. Who processes it

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram): to deliver messages and ad lead forms.
  • Google (Google Sheets): where our team keeps its list of enquiries.
  • Telegram: to alert our team when a new enquiry arrives.
  • Cloudflare: secure connection between WhatsApp and our systems.

These providers process data only to provide their service to us. Some of them may store data outside India.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your enquiry for as long as it is active, and delete it within 24 months of our last conversation unless you buy a plot, in which case we keep records the law requires us to keep.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Stop messages: reply STOP on WhatsApp at any time. We will not message you again.
  • Delete your data: reply DELETE on WhatsApp, or contact us below. We erase your personal data within 7 days.
  • You can also ask us what data we hold about you, or ask us to correct it.

6. Security

Access to enquiry data is limited to our team and protected by passwords. WhatsApp messages are delivered through Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform.
